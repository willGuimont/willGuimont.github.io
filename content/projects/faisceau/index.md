faisceau brings data structures and algorithms into the browser through WebAssembly. The collection covers stacks, queues, linked lists, heaps, and trees, alongside sorting, searching, hashing, matrix multiplication, and maximum-subarray algorithms.

Explore different approaches to the same problem, from chaining and open addressing in hash tables to naive, recursive, and Strassen matrix multiplication.
