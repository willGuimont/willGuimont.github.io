wigum::about

William Guimont-Martin

Québec, Canada

I'm a researcher, programmer, and educator. I'm currently pursuing a Ph.D. in computer science at Université Laval, working at the intersection of robotics, artificial intelligence, and computer vision.

Full CVPapersProjects

What I work on

I’m interested in how robots perceive the world, and how we can build models that hold up outside the conditions they were trained in. My research explores using additional sensor modalities, such as hyperspectral, polar, and LiDAR data, during training to learn more robust and generalizable representations.

I also build software to explore ideas: graphics experiments, algorithm visualizations, electronics and robotics libraries, and small apps and games. My interests span computer science, mathematics, computer graphics, and AI. You can find the work on my Projects and Papers pages.

Selected experience

Research

At Norlab, I’ve worked on perception using point clouds, forest imagery, and robot sensor data. For my master’s research, I introduced MaskBEV, an approach to detecting objects and completing their footprints in bird’s-eye-view 3D point clouds. I’ve also contributed to tree segmentation and species classification with SilvaScenes, and terrain classification with BorealTC.

Teaching

I’ve been a teaching assistant in deep learning, mobile robotics, operating systems, and software engineering at Université Laval. I design laboratories and teaching material as well as support students. In Winter 2025, I took responsibility for the deep learning course apart from lectures.

I also developed workshops on Bash, Docker, testing, refactoring, and test-driven development. I like teaching principles that remain useful as the tools change. The workshop approach is described in Teaching Software Development Skills.

Industry

My work has included computer vision and automated production lines at Devoray, ultrasonic-data analysis at Olympus NDT Canada, and computer vision and photogrammetry at Bentley Systems. At Bentley, I developed an algorithm for detecting and modelling aerial cables from images, which contributed to a U.S. patent.

Robotics mentoring

I’ve mentored students in robotics and programming with Team 5440 Les Chevaliers at École secondaire de La Seigneurie since 2016. The team competed in FIRST Robotics Competition through 2025 and competes in CRC Robotics from the 2026 season onward. Teaching and building robots with students have been a substantial part of my life alongside university research.

Education and selected honours

All three degrees are at Université Laval:

  • Ph.D. in Computer Science, 2023–present.
  • M.Sc. in Computer Science, 2021–2023.
  • B.Eng. in Software Engineering, Distinction Profile, 2017–2021.

My research has been recognized with the NSERC Canada Graduate Scholarship — Doctoral and an FRQNT Doctoral Research Scholarship. I also received the Fondation Famille Choquette Scientific Leadership Scholarship for academic achievement and community involvement, including robotics mentoring.
